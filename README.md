# PerishABit- Perishable Food Tracker 

# Overview 

PerishABit is an app targeted to help young adults, particularly college students, track perishable food items in their refrigerator and prioritize the most important
food items to use. It is designed to reduce food waste by encouraging young adults to develop organization habits. 

# Features 

1) Add, update, and delete food items
2) Track expiration dates and quantities
3) View top 3 food items approaching expiration
4) Persistent food data between app sessions 



# Tech Stack

1) Swift
2) SwiftUI
3) SwiftData

1) Search feature to find food items inside refrigerator
2) Sorting by dates and categories inside refrigerator
